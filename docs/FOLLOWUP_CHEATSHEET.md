## Do

- Value every touch · one CTA · one primary link · ≤120 words  
- Pause sequence on any reply · human-send hot replies  
- Mirror their language/channel · L0–L1 personalization only unless verified L2  
- Breakup then **stop** · honor unsub instantly  
- Beachhead first wave only (Athens × influencers)

## Don’t

- “Just bumping / circling back” empty · guilt · fake “saw you opened”  
- Daily FUs · multi-channel same day · 6th cold email  
- Argue with “no” · Instant desperate discounts · PDF decks cold  
- Auto-send conversational replies · Fabricate personalization

## Cadence (cold email)

| Step | Day | Angle | CTA idea |
|------|-----|-------|----------|
| E1 | 0 | Pain (DMs) | Worth a look? |
| E2 | 2–3 | Product (offers) | Which offer first? |
| E3 | 5–6 | Proof (kit) | 15-min map? |
| E4 | 8–9 | Contrast (managers) | Pricing + included? |
| E5 | 12–14 | Breakup | Close or book? |

Tue–Thu preferred · max 5 cold · after E5 silence → optional 1 DM then exhausted 90–180d
