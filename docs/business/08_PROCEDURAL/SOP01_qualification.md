# SOP-01 — Ideal client qualification

| Meta | Value |
|------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Decide fast if a lead fits the kit ICP. |
| **Owner role** | Sales / audit |
| **Last updated** | 2026-10-31 |
| **Status** | `active` |
| **Non-goals** | Closing on the first message; serving anti-ICP. |

**Trigger:** New lead (form, referral, warm intro)  
**Inputs:** Niche, city, site/IG, contact  
**Outputs:** Fit / Soft fit / No-fit + next step  
**SLA:** Qualify within 24 h of lead  

### Steps
1. Confirm niche in ICP list  
2. Confirm real clients/traffic signals  
3. Confirm weak/outdated/absent digital presence  
4. Budget reality check (kit band €790+)  
5. Decision maker reachable?  

### Quality bar
Can answer “why kit / why now” in one line.

### Failure modes
- Chasing startups needing SaaS  
- Ignoring no-budget signals
