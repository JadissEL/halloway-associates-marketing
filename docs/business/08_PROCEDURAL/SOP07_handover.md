# SOP-07 — Handover + training

| Meta | Value |
|------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Client can operate site/contact without us daily. |
| **Owner role** | Delivery · Success |
| **Last updated** | 2026-10-31 |
| **Status** | `active` |
| **Non-goals** | Full CMS training for unscoped admin. |

**Trigger:** Site live  
**Inputs:** Live URL, credentials notes (if any), captions, Google copy  
**Outputs:** Handover note + 15-min call optional  
**SLA:** Same day as launch or next business day  

### Steps
1. Send URL + how to share (bio, QR, Google)  
2. Deliver 5 captions + Google texts  
3. Show where leads arrive (email/WhatsApp/form)  
4. Offer care plan once  
5. Ask CSAT 1–5  

### Quality bar
Client posts link within 7 days or documents blocker.

### Failure modes
- Dumping files without explanation
