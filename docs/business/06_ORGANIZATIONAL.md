## Hats (one person may wear many)

| Hat | Mission |
|-----|---------|
| **Founder / CEO** | Standards, switches, strategy, money gates |
| **Sales / audit** | Qualification, 15-min audit, proposals |
| **Delivery / web production** | Intake → build → QA → launch |
| **Success / care** | Monthly care loop, CSAT, referrals |
| **Ops / finance admin** | Invoices, folders, close, tools |
| **Growth systems** | Outbound OS owner — **role exists, launch gated** |

---

## Hiring / subcontract triggers

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| WIP > 3 kits or evenings & weekends > 2 weeks | Subcontract design or front-end slices |
| Care clients > 8 and response SLA slips | VA for scheduling / content updates |
| Outbound unlocked and volume rises | Specialist copy/ops — still caps & compliance |

Always: written brief, fixed price or capped hours, QA by Delivery hat.
