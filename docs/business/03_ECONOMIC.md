## Contribution vs fixed

- **Contribution** = Deal − direct delivery cost (time + contractor + stock photos if any)  
- **Fixed** = tools, domain, accounting, insurance, founder draw floor  

Break-even kits/month ≈ `Fixed monthly ÷ contribution per kit`  
Example: fixed €800 / contrib €600 ⇒ **~2 kits/month** break-even (illustrative).

## Expansion economics (order)

1. **Retainer expansion** (care) — highest leverage on existing trust  
2. **Niche expansion** in same city — reuse local reputation  
3. **City expansion** — only after one niche playbook works

## What not to sell yet

- Custom native apps / multi-tenant SaaS  
- Marketplaces  
- Heavy retainers without SOPs and WIP limits  
- “Anything for anyone” projects that break the kit factory
