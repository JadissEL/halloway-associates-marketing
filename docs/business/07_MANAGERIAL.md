## Weekly operating cadence

| Day | Focus |
|-----|--------|
| **Mon** | Plan: pipeline + WIP + money (30–45 min) — use `weekly_ops_review.md` |
| **Tue–Thu** | Delivery deep work; max 1 sales/audit slot/day |
| **Fri** | Review: quality, invoices, care, update scorecard; check `LAUNCH_SWITCH` still OFF |

---

## KPI dashboard (definitions)

| KPI | Definition |
|-----|------------|
| Pipeline | # leads in new/contacted/audit booked |
| Delivery WIP | # kits between intake and launch |
| Cash | Cleared balance − unpaid tools (simple) |
| CSAT | 1–5 after launch (ask once) |
| Margin | (Revenue − contractor − explicit costs) / Revenue |
| Care MRR | Sum of active care fees |

---

## Scorecard — leading vs lagging

| Leading | Lagging |
|---------|---------|
| Audits booked this week | Kits launched this month |
| Proposals sent | Revenue recognized |
| Intake completeness % | Margin |
| WIP age (days) | CSAT |
| `LAUNCH_SWITCH` still OFF (compliance) | Care churn |

---

## Meeting rhythms (solo)

- **Mon self-plan** (template)  
- **Fri self-review** (template)  
- **Monthly close** (accounting checklist)  

No vanity meetings.

---

## Escalation rules

| Situation | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Scope creep | Written change order + price/time; else refuse |
| Unpaid invoice > 14 days | Pause delivery/care; one reminder; then hold files |
| Quality fail (your bar) | Do not launch; fix or refund per policy |
| Burnout signals | Cap WIP at 2; defer sales for 1 week |

---

## Anti-burnout constraints

- Max **45 h/week** intended  
- No client calls after 19:00 local without exception log  
- One full rest day / week  
- WIP hard cap: **3** active builds
