## Why we exist

Local businesses lose clients to weak or missing digital presence: outdated sites, Instagram-only bios, broken contact forms.  
We exist to give them a **clear, bookable, sharable** presence — fast — so opportunity stops leaking.

*(Internal product lineage once called “LocalPresence”; public entity is **Halloway Associates**, offer **Kit présence digitale**.)*

---

## What “good work” means

- Client can explain the site in one sentence  
- A stranger can contact/book in one tap on mobile  
- Desktop (≥1024px) feels like a real business platform, not a stretched mobile app  
- Delivery finishes in the promised window when intake is complete  
- We would proudly show the work as a reference  

---

## Principles

1. **Preserve > Extend > Optimize** — don’t rewrite working product; extend carefully; polish last.  
2. **Productized delivery over custom chaos** — kit + niche skins, not endless bespoke.  
3. **Respect local businesses** — their time, budget, and reputation matter more than our ego.  
4. **No spam ethics** — consent, relevance, unsubscribe, honest copy; Outbound OS stays locked until switch ON.  
5. **Concrete over hype** — no “AI revolution” theater; show hours, prices, CTAs.  
6. **Standards over volume** — fewer excellent kits beat many cheap disasters.

| Refuse | Why |

| Deceptive outreach | Burns trust and legal risk |

---

## Decision filter

> Does this increase **client outcomes** and **founder leverage** without lowering standards?

If no → don’t do it. If only leverage but lowers standards → don’t. If only ego → don’t.
